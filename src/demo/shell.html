Rules and activity

Rules gives a site a daily budget and shows how much of it today has already spent. Activity is the day itself, minute by minute — hover the chart to see what you were on, and put a limit on anything from the list without leaving the tab. Both tabs below are the extension's real dashboard.

When a limit runs out

The YouTube budget above is already spent, so this is what the next youtube.com tab would show instead of the video. You are not locked out: take five more minutes, ten, or type your own, and Continue goes back to the page. Extend it here and the limit on the Rules tab moves with it — both screens read the same store.
